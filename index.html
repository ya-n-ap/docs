    Welcome to YANAP!

    TL;DR  too lang; please read    ¯\(ツ)/¯

    YANAP is a combined hardware and software solution that blocks ads, tracking, phishing, and malware in home and small business LAN (Local Area Network) environments.

    YANAP stands for "You Are Not A Product".

    The intention behind YANAP is to offer a remarkably simple solution for those without an IT background or the spare time to deal with technical complexity, helping them to easily eliminate as many of the intrusive and harmful aspects of the Internet as possible. At its core, the project aims to empower people to reclaim their digital rights and protect their privacy, security, and identity from misuse and abuse.

    Software - Thanks to FOSS (Free and Open Source Software)

    YANAP is built on four key FOSS projects: DietPi, AdGuard Home, Docker & Docker Compose, and MkDocs Material, and many Contributors who maintain the Filters.

    These are the real core components of YANAP. What I’ve done is simply create some lightweight scripts1 to make them self-configuring and self-maintaining, so they can better adapt to your LAN environment.

      DietPi

    a lightweight and highly optimized Debian-based Linux operating system, designed for single-board computers (SBCs) and low-resource devices. It enables this project to run on minimal SBC hardware stably with only a few small tweaks. For you, the user, hat means you won't need to spend a lot of money.
