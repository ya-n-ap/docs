    FRITZ!Box  

    The information shown in the screenshots is for reference only. Please follow the tutorial and enter the information shown on your own YANAP page.

    The interface on your router may look slightly different depending on your hardware model and firmware version, but the overall process remains the same.

    Tip

    • Don’t worry about messing things up. You can always write down the original values in Step 3. If something goes wrong, just change them back and reboot your router. Everything will work again.


    Step 1 - Log In to Your FRITZ!Box

    Click the link of Your Router's Web Interface➊ on Device Info Page to open Your Router's Settings Page➋. Enter your Password➌ and click Log In➍.

    • If you don’t know the password, check the label on your router for the default password.

    • If you’ve changed the default password but forgot it, the only option is to reset your router.

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 2 - Find the Setting Page

    Once logged in, you will see a page similar to the screenshot below, click Internet➊ from the left sidebar.

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 3 - Configure Your DNS Server

    On this screen, follow these steps:

    1. Open the DNS configuration panel:

      • click Type of Connection➊    →    click DNS Server➋

    2. Set your own DNS server addresses:

      • activate Use other DNSv4 servers➌    →    enter IP Address of your DNSv4 server➍ into Preferred DNSv4 Server➎ and Alternative DNSv4 Server➎

      • activate Use other DNSv6 servers➏    →    enter IP Address of your DNSv6 server➐ into Preferred DNSv6 Server➑ and Alternative DNSv6 Server➑

    3. Allow backup DNS just in case:

      • activate Fallback to public DNS servers when DNS disrupted➒

      This ensures your router will use the ISP’s DNS if your YANAP server is unavailable.

    4. Apply the changes:

      • click Apply➓

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 4 - Confirm Your Configuration

    After clicking Apply, you may or may not see the screen below. If it appears, follow the on-screen instructions. In most cases, the easiest and most user-friendly way is Confirm with FRITZ!Box button.

    You need to click No telephone? Confirm with FRITZ!Box button or app to get more instructions. By default, the FRITZ!Box shows confirmation instructions for telephone only.

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 5 - Final Confirmation

    You have to confirm one more time by clicking OK.

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 6 - Reboot Your Router

    Reboot your FRITZ!Box to make sure the changes take effect.


    Step 7 - Reconnect Your Devices1

    To make sure everything works right away, you need to reconnect your devices to the internet.

    • If your device connects to the internet via Wi-Fi: Turn Wi-Fi Off then Turn Wi-Fi On.
    • If your device connects to the internet using an Ethernet cable: Unplug the Ethernet Cable then Plug in the Ethernet Cable.
    • If your device connects to the internet via Wi-Fi but has no option to Turn Wi-Fi off / Turn Wi-Fi On: Just restart the device.


    Done - Test & Move On

    From now on, you should see significantly fewer ads, trackers, and pop-ups. Try visiting an ad-heavy website to check if the blocking are working.

    All set? Great - now head to Manage Blocking.


    1. This step is optional, but recommended. Some devices don’t refresh their settings automatically after a router reboot, so reconnecting helps ensure everything works as expected. ↩
