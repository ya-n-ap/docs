    Tips before Starting

    The set values (e.g., IP address) shown in the screenshots are for reference only. Please follow the tutorial and enter the information shown on your own YANAP page.

    The interface on your router may look slightly different depending on your hardware model and firmware version, but the overall process remains the same.

    Tip

    Don’t worry about messing things up. You can always write down the original values in Step 3. If something goes wrong, just change them back and reboot your router. Everything will work again.


    Step 1 - Log In to Your ASUS Router

    Click the link of Your Router's Web Interface➊ on Device Info Page to open Your Router's Settings Page➋. Enter your Username & Password➌ and click Sign In➍.

    • If you don’t know the password, check the label on your router for the default password.

    • If you’ve changed the default password but forgot it, the only option is to reset your router.

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 2 - Find the Setting Page

    Once logged in, you will see a page similar to the screenshot below, click LAN➊ from the left sidebar.

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 3 - Configure Your DNS Server

    On this screen, follow these steps:

    1. Open the DNS configuration panel:

      • click DHCP Server➊

    2. Set your own DNS server addresses:

      • enter IP Address of your DNSv4 server➋ into DNS Server 1➌

      • enter IP Address of your DNSv6 server➍ into IPv6 DNS Server➎

        (If you don’t see IPv6 DNS Server, just skip this step.)

    3. Switch Off This Option:

      • ensure Advertise router's IP in addition to user-specified DNS is set to No➏

    4. Apply the changes:

      • click Apply➐

    Click the image to enlarge.


    Step 4 - Reboot Your Router

    Reboot your ASUS router to make sure the changes take effect.


    Step 5 - Reconnect Your Devices1

    To make sure everything works right away, you need to reconnect your devices to the internet.

    • If your device connects to the internet via Wi-Fi: Turn Wi-Fi Off then Turn Wi-Fi On.
    • If your device connects to the internet using an Ethernet cable: Unplug the Ethernet Cable then Plug in the Ethernet Cable.
    • If your device connects to the internet via Wi-Fi but has no option to Turn Wi-Fi off / Turn Wi-Fi On: Just restart the device.

    From now on, you should see significantly fewer ads, trackers, and pop-ups. Try visiting an ad-heavy website to check if the blocking are working.

    All set? Great - now head to Manage Blocking.


    1. This step is optional, but recommended. Some devices don’t refresh their settings automatically after a router reboot, so reconnecting helps ensure everything works as expected. ↩
